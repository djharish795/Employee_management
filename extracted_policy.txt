LEAVE, WORK FROM HOME (WFH) & HOLIDAY POLICY
Policy Period: 1 July 2026 – 30 June 2027

1. Purpose
The purpose of this policy is to establish a clear, fair, and transparent framework governing Leave, Work From Home (WFH), and Holiday benefits for all employees of Naprocs Technologies. This policy aims to ensure business continuity while promoting employee well-being, accountability, and efficient workforce management.
2. Casual Leave (CL)
2.1 Leave Entitlement
Every employee shall be entitled to 1.5 (One and a Half) Casual Leave (CL) days per month.
The total annual entitlement shall be 18 Casual Leave days during the policy period.
2.2 Prior Intimation
All leave requests must be submitted at least 24 hours in advance.
Casual Leave requested on the same day shall not be approved under normal circumstances.
2.3 Emergency / Unplanned Leave
Unplanned leave shall be considered only in genuine emergency situations.
Such leave shall require approval from the Chief Technology Officer (CTO) or the Chief Executive Officer (CEO) (in absence of CTO for time being) based on project requirements, business priorities, and resource availability.
2.4 Late Arrival
Employees who expect to report late to the office must inform their Project Lead at least 2 hours before their scheduled reporting time.
Frequent late arrivals without a valid reason may result in disciplinary action.
2.5 Half-Day Leave
For employees following the standard office timings of 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM:
Employees availing a first-half leave must report to the office on or before 1:30 PM.
Reporting after 1:30 PM shall be considered as a Full-Day Leave unless otherwise approved by the Management.
3. Leave Carry Forward
Employees are encouraged to utilize their Casual Leave during the policy period.
Unused leave shall expire on 30 June 2027.
However, a maximum of 7 unused Casual Leave days may be carried forward to the next policy year.
Any balance exceeding 7 days shall automatically lapse.
4. Work From Home (WFH) Policy
General Policy
Naprocs Technologies follows a Work From Office (WFO) culture. Therefore, Work From Home (WFH) shall not be considered a regular employee benefit and shall only be permitted under exceptional circumstances.

Eligibility
Every employee may avail a maximum of One (1) Work From Home day per month.
Prior approval from the Project Lead is mandatory.
The HR Department must be informed before availing WFH.
Project-Level Restriction
The total Work From Home days for an entire project team shall not exceed Three (3) days in a calendar month.
Responsibility of the Project Lead
The Project Lead shall be responsible for:
Proper work allocation.
Resource planning.
Ensuring uninterrupted project execution during an employee's absence due to Leave or Work From Home.
5. Overtime Policy
Naprocs Technologies recognizes that certain business situations may require employees to work beyond the regular working hours. Employees are expected to extend their full cooperation in such circumstances to ensure timely delivery of projects and business commitments.
5.1 Applicability
Employees may be required to work overtime under, but not limited to, the following circumstances:
Project deadlines and critical delivery schedules.
Client commitments and urgent business requirements.
Production deployments, maintenance activities, or issue resolution.
Client visits, business meetings, demonstrations, or presentations.
Company events, training programs, recruitment drives, exhibitions, or official functions.
Any other business requirement as determined by the Management.
5.2 Employee Responsibility
Employees are expected to cooperate with the Management and their respective Project Leads whenever overtime is required for official business purposes.
Overtime shall be treated as an extension of the employee's professional responsibilities and shall be performed diligently and professionally.
Employees shall ensure timely completion of assigned work during overtime hours.
5.3 Exemption from Overtime
Employees seeking exemption from overtime due to genuine personal or medical emergencies must obtain prior approval from the Chief Technology Officer (CTO).
In the temporary absence of the CTO, such approval may be granted only by the Chief Executive Officer (CEO).
Without such approval, employees shall be expected to participate in the scheduled overtime whenever required.
5.4 Management Rights
The Management reserves the right to schedule, modify, or cancel overtime requirements based on project priorities, client commitments, operational requirements, and business needs. Employees are expected to comply with such requirements in the interest of the organization.
Note: Unless specifically communicated otherwise by the Management in writing, overtime performed under this policy shall not automatically entitle an employee to overtime wages, compensatory off, or any additional benefits. Any such benefits, if applicable, shall be governed by the Company's compensation policy or applicable law.

6. Maternity Leave
Naprocs Technologies is committed to supporting employees during maternity.
Female employees shall be eligible for up to 12 weeks of Maternity Leave, subject to approval by the Management.
In exceptional medical or personal circumstances, an additional extension of up to 12 weeks may be granted with the approval of the Management based on supporting medical documentation and business requirements.
Where the provisions of the applicable law provide greater maternity benefits than this policy, the applicable law shall prevail, and the employee shall receive benefits in accordance with such law.
The Company reserves the right to request supporting medical certificates or other relevant documentation wherever necessary.

9. General Guidelines
All Leave and Work From Home (WFH) requests shall be submitted only through the official company email. Every request must be addressed to the HR Department, Project Lead, and General Manager – Operations & Business. Requests communicated through phone calls, WhatsApp, SMS, or any other communication platform shall not be treated as official requests. In emergency situations, employees must immediately inform their reporting manager through a phone call and submit the official email request at the earliest possible opportunity.
Employees shall ensure proper handover of all assigned work before proceeding on Leave or Work From Home.
The Project Lead shall be responsible for ensuring proper work allocation, resource planning, and continuity of project deliverables during the employee's absence.
Approval of Leave and Work From Home requests shall be subject to project requirements, client commitments, operational priorities, and resource availability. The Management reserves the right to approve, defer, or reject any request.
Any misuse of Leave or Work From Home privileges, submission of false information, unauthorized absence, or violation of this policy may result in disciplinary action as per the Company's Code of Conduct.
The HR Department reserves the right to verify emergency leave requests and may request supporting documents whenever necessary.
Any matter not specifically covered under this policy shall be decided by the Management of Naprocs Technologies, whose decision shall be final and binding.
This policy shall come into effect on 1 July 2026 and shall remain valid until 30 June 2027, unless amended, revised, or withdrawn by the Management.
Approval
